# FOS-0317 Legal Reference Normalization Policy Model

Status: accepted.

## Decision

Define deterministic normalization policies for legal references before resolution.

## Normalization Areas

- Raw legal reference text.
- Citation form.
- Structural anchor.
- Jurisdiction and authority hints.
- Target descriptor.
- Canonical reference representation.

## Boundary

Normalization prepares references; it does not resolve targets or infer legal meaning.
