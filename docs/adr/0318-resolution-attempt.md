# FOS-0318 Legal Reference Resolution Attempt Model

Status: accepted.

## Decision

Define resolution attempt records for deterministic attempts to resolve legal references.

## Outcomes

- Resolved.
- Unresolved.
- Ambiguous.
- Failed.
- Cancelled.
- Corrected or superseded later through additive records.

## Requirements

Attempts reference legal reference identity, normalization policy, resolution policy, target corpus snapshot, and audit context.
